About Me

FAQ

Things people
usually ask.

Answers to common questions about working with me, how I build, and what to expect.

We start by getting aligned. I'll gather your requirements, understand what you're building and why, then we settle on the right stack together. Being on the same page from day one saves a lot of back and forth later.
Most projects take about a month from start to launch. That includes the build, feedback rounds, bug fixes, and small tweaks. Bigger or more complex work may take longer but we'll agree on a timeline upfront.
I'm based in Malaysia but work fully remote. Timezone, location, none of that is a barrier. If you have an idea and an internet connection, we can work together.
I take a deposit upfront to kick things off, and the remaining balance is due once the project is delivered. Simple and fair for both sides.
A clear brief goes a long way — what you want to build, who it's for, and any references help. That said, requirements can evolve and I'm flexible enough to adjust along the way.
I'm open to most things — web apps, websites, dashboards, AI integrations. The one thing I'm upfront about is I work best on projects that aren't overly complex from the start. Good things take time, and I'd rather do one thing well than rush something big.
